# Warm food photography

These three illustrative food images were created with the built-in image generation tool for this demo restaurant, then encoded as WebP for delivery. They are not photographs of actual Cashless dishes.

Shared brief: premium editorial food photography, cream ceramic plate, dark walnut table, warm amber side lighting, blurred forest-green background, landscape composition, no text or logos.

Subjects:
- chicken-warm.webp: herb roasted chicken leg, white rice and seasonal greens.
- pasta-warm.webp: tomato and herb tagliatelle, parmesan and basil.
- spring-rolls-warm.webp: four crispy vegetable rolls and sweet chilli dipping sauce.

Existing Unsplash image credits remain in sources.json.
